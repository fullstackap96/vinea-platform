## Product Thesis

Vinea should become the Catholic parish operating system for daily staff work. Its wedge is not generic church management breadth, but closed-loop Catholic operations: requests, follow-up, families, records, documents, calendars, communications, and safe AI.

## Built Product Surface

- Marketing site and demo request.
- Public intake: baptism, funeral, wedding, OCIA, join parish.
- Staff dashboard and role work hub.
- Request list/detail workflows.
- People and households with duplicate review.
- Sacramental records and baptism certificates.
- Mass intentions.
- Imports.
- Reports.
- Communications center and email support.
- Google Calendar integration.
- Documents and family portal tokens.
- Settings, onboarding, staff access.
- Audit log, exports, export audit reviewer prototype.
- AI summaries and reply drafts.

## Product Strengths

- Catholic-specific request and record workflows.
- Strong "what needs attention today" direction.
- Request-to-record continuity.
- Safety-first AI and export posture.
- Multi-parish/diocesan strategy already being prepared.
- Strong trust-center and QA evidence culture.

## Product Gaps

- No production-ready pricing/packaging in repo docs.
- No public trust center yet.
- Certificates beyond baptism are not complete.
- Record notations/corrections are missing.
- Workflow automation is not yet no-code.
- Mobile/PWA staff surface is not first-class.
- Communications lack SMS, inbound threading, and template depth.
- Religious education/OCIA workspace is not yet deep enough.
- Migration tools need competitor-specific adapters.

## Product Roadmap Focus
