## Common Workflow Pain Points

- Parish office intake appears to route through general inboxes, secretary inboxes, or contact forms.
- Sacramental and certificate workflows are likely spread across staff roles, especially at parish-school communities.
- Bulletin, registration, PSR, and ministry forms suggest separate workflows that may not share one status view.
- Larger parishes have many staff and ministries, which increases handoff and visibility risk.
- Smaller parishes likely need lightweight tracking without adding administrative overhead.

## Recommended Outreach Sequence

- Batch 1: send ranks 1-8 only. These have the strongest role fit and operational complexity.
- Batch 2: send ranks 9-17 three business days later if capacity allows.
- Batch 3: send ranks 18-25 only after reviewing replies and sharpening the message.
- Follow-up 1: 5 business days after first email, short and helpful, no guilt.
- Follow-up 2: 8-10 business days later, offer to route to the right staff member and then stop.
- Weekly cap: 8-12 new parish emails per week to preserve quality and avoid spam-like behavior.
